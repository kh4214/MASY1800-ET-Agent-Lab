# Lab 8 - Self-Reflection

Through this lab, I learned that evaluating emerging technologies requires more than looking at their popularity or capabilities. When analyzing LLM coding assistants, I realized that vendor claims about productivity are not enough. We also need independent evidence and comparisons with competing tools.

One interesting finding was that both the 1,000-employee SaaS company and the four-person startup received a Trial recommendation, but for different reasons. The larger company needed to focus on security, governance, and integration, while the startup needed to consider costs, code review capacity, and vendor dependency. This helped me understand that different organizations can make the same decision based on different priorities.

I also learned the importance of continuously monitoring technology changes instead of treating adoption as a one-time decision. Overall, this lab helped me better connect technology landscape analysis with practical management decisions.
