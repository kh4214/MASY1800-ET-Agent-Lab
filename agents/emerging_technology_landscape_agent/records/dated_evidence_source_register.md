## Cross-source interpretation

Vendor material is suitable for feature, price, and contract monitoring, not for independent productivity claims. Independent studies should be compared by task, developer experience, codebase familiarity, tool version, and measurement method before influencing a management decision. Standards and regulatory sources frame controls and applicability; they do not replace legal advice. The most hype-sensitive claim is that LLM coding assistants universally increase developer productivity or ROI: the evidence above is mixed, rapidly dated, and non-transferable without local measurement.
