# Self Reflection Draft for Student Review

This is a draft for the student to review and personalize. It does not claim personal experiences or learning outcomes.

The Lab 8 agent separates general technology signals from the decisions that matter for each organization. It uses dated sources, identifies uncertainty, and does not treat vendor claims or media attention as proof of value. The two tests kept the technology and application constant but changed the management priorities. The larger SaaS company needs stronger coordination and governance. The smaller startup needs to focus on review capacity, cost, and supplier dependence. Both cases support a conditional Trial rather than automatic adoption. Before final submission, the student should review the source choices, test evidence, and any personal reflection language.
