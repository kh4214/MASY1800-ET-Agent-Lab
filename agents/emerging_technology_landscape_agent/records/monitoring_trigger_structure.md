# Monitoring Trigger Structure

| Watch item | Refresh cadence | Decision-relevant trigger | Management response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Competitor capabilities, plan terms, and pricing | Monthly | A material change affects viable alternatives, supplier concentration, data handling, or cost assumptions. | Recompare shortlisted options and update the posture assumptions; involve procurement/security when relevant. |
| Independent productivity and quality evidence | Quarterly | New evidence is sufficiently relevant to the team's task types, codebase conditions, and tool version to challenge the current evidence assessment. | Reassess evidence strength and decide whether a controlled local evaluation is warranted. |
| Industry adoption and ecosystem developments | Quarterly | A credible shift changes interoperability, developer workflow, or availability of complementary controls. | Refresh the landscape position and alternative/complement comparison. |
| Overall landscape position | Quarterly | Cross-source evidence changes the contextual rationale for Adopt, Trial, Assess, or Hold. | Revisit the recommendation and document what changed. |
| Security, regulatory, supplier, or major service developments | Event-driven | A material incident, policy/contract change, service disruption, enforcement development, or new official guidance is relevant to the intended use. | Pause, scope, or adjust evaluation/deployment and obtain appropriate security, legal, privacy, or vendor review. |

No universal quantitative threshold is specified. A trigger is decision-relevant only when its scope and evidence can be connected to the stated application and organization.
