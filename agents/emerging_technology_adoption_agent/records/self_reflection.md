# Lab 6 Self Reflection

This lab helped me clarify and better articulate my approach to emerging technology adoption. My fundamental perspective did not change significantly, but I developed a clearer understanding of how to evaluate adoption decisions from a business perspective rather than focusing primarily on technical risks.

One important takeaway was that delaying adoption can also create risks, especially for startups operating in competitive markets. I believe that small companies should consider the potential benefits of faster development, lower costs, and earlier market entry alongside the risks of experimentation. Limited resources do not necessarily justify a more conservative adoption strategy.

The comparison between an established SaaS company and a four-person startup also helped me distinguish the cost of experimentation from the consequences of failure. I became more conscious of the importance of defining acceptable quality and safety standards, using existing technology infrastructure when appropriate, and evaluating whether an experiment creates meaningful business value.

Overall, this lab reinforced my belief that technology adoption should be proactive but controlled. In my future professional practice, I would prioritize identifying opportunities, setting clear minimum standards, and making timely decisions based on business value and available evidence rather than organizational size alone.
